# 5-Minute Client Demo Script: Himal Estates
**Pitch to the Real-Estate Middleman: "Why Himal Estates Beats Social Media Alone"**

---

## 🎯 Target Audience
The client is an independent real estate & business dealmaker in Nepal who currently conducts all business through Facebook groups, TikTok posts, and chaotic WhatsApp chats.

## ⏱ Time Allocation: 5 Minutes
- **Minute 0:00 - 1:00**: The Problem with Social Media vs The Homepage Solution
- **Minute 1:00 - 2:00**: Property Search, Filters, Nepali Units & Interactive Map
- **Minute 2:00 - 3:00**: High-Conversion Listing Detail, Lightbox & Instant Inquiry
- **Minute 3:00 - 4:00**: Middleman Admin CRM: Leads, Status Pipeline & Approvals
- **Minute 4:00 - 5:00**: Commission Tracker & "Share to Social" Asset Generator

---

## 🎬 Step-by-Step Script & Walkthrough

### Part 1: Homepage & First Impressions (Minute 0:00 - 1:00)
**Action:** Open Homepage on desktop or tablet view. Show language toggle (EN ⇄ नेपाली).
> **Talking Point:** *"Right now, when you post a property on Facebook or TikTok, it gets buried in someone's feed after 48 hours. When a buyer asks you 'Do you have a 3BHK flat in Baneshwor under 35,000?', you have to scroll through hundreds of old camera roll photos or old posts. Look at Himal Estates: Every single property you manage is permanently cataloged, indexed, and searchable 24/7."*

**Highlight:**
- Himalayan brand palette (calm teal, terracotta accent, snowy clean background).
- Search bar with Category, Location (Kathmandu, Lalitpur, Pokhara), and Price filter.
- Verified Listings & Fast Response badges building instant authority.

---

### Part 2: Listings Catalog & The Power of Discovery (Minute 1:00 - 2:00)
**Action:** Click **"Explore Listings"** or search "Flats for Rent in Kathmandu". Toggle **Grid View** ➔ **Interactive Map View**.
> **Talking Point:** *"Social media has no filters. Here, your clients filter by exact Nepali units: Ropani, Aana, bedroom count, or parking facilities. Even better: look at this interactive map. A buyer relocating to Sanepa or Lazimpat can explore exact neighborhoods on OpenStreetMap."*

**Highlight:**
- Accurate Nepali prices (`NPR 25,000/month`, `NPR 3.5 Crore`, `NPR 45 Lakh/Aana`).
- "Hot Deal" and "Verified" tags.
- Heart button for saving favorites into `/saved`.

---

### Part 3: High-Conversion Property Page & Inquiries (Minute 2:00 - 3:00)
**Action:** Click on a top listing (e.g. *Modern 3BHK Furnished Apartment in Jhamsikhel*).
> **Talking Point:** *"When people see your Facebook post, they comment 'Price please' or 'Inbox'. Half of them never respond. On your website, they get the full story: an HD photo lightbox, exact floor area, water supply, generator backup, and road width. And look at this bottom action bar: they can call you in one tap, message you directly on WhatsApp with the property ID already typed, or send a structured inquiry."*

**Interactive Demo Actions:**
- Click **Call**: Show simulated dialing modal ("Calling Himal Estates Agent... Connected 00:14").
- Click **Chat**: Open floating live chat simulation.
- Click **WhatsApp**: Show how it generates a formatted message with property title and price.

---

### Part 4: The Middleman's Back Office (/admin) (Minute 3:00 - 4:15)
**Action:** Navigate to `/admin` and log in with `admin@himalestates.com` / `demo2024`.
> **Talking Point:** *"This is your command center. You no longer need to write deals down in sticky notes or search through disorganized WhatsApp chats. In your Admin Dashboard, you see:
> 1. How many active listings you have.
> 2. Today's fresh inquiries in your Leads CRM pipeline.
> 3. Pending listings submitted by property owners waiting for your approval."*

**Interactive Demo Actions:**
- Move a lead from **"New"** to **"Contacted"** to **"Negotiating"**.
- Review the **Approval Queue**: show a new room/shop listing submitted by a Kathmandu landlord, and click **"Approve Listing"** to push it live to the public website with one click.

---

### Part 5: Commission Tracker & Social Media Superweapon (Minute 4:15 - 5:00)
**Action:** Click **"Commissions"** and then **"Share to Social"**.
> **Talking Point:** *"We are not telling you to stop using TikTok or Facebook — we are making your social media 10x faster. Look at this 'Share to Social' tool: You pick any property, and in one click, Himal Estates generates a high-definition branded flyer for your Instagram/Facebook story or TikTok banner, with your logo, phone number, and pre-written Nepali caption ready to copy! And when the deal closes, your Commission Tracker records the transaction value, calculates your 2% middleman cut, and tracks your monthly earnings with instant CSV export."*

**Closing Hook:**
> *"With Himal Estates, you don't look like an informal broker. You look like Nepal's premier digital property agency. You own your brand, you own your audience, and you close deals faster."*
